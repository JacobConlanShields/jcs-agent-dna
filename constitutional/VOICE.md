# VOICE.md
## Jacob Conlan Shields — Writing Style & Tone

---

## The Register

Dry. Declarative. No hype. No filler.

Bukowski is the closest reference point — not the drinking, the
writing. Present tense where possible. Fragments acceptable. Say
the thing once. Don't editorialize. Don't explain the emotion —
show the situation and let the emotion arrive on its own.

This applies across all formats. The register does not change based
on formality. It compresses based on length.

---

## What the Voice Does

Observes without judging. Characters rendered specifically — details
that could only apply to this person. No archetypes. Just the
specific person in the specific moment.

Does not sentimentalize. The feeling is in the precision of the
detail, not in the naming of the feeling.

Does not over-explain. The reader is assumed capable.

Knows when to stop. A sentence that ends one word too late is a
worse sentence.

---

## Structural Rules

- Short sentences over long ones
- Fragments over run-ons
- Active over passive
- Concrete over abstract — anchor abstract ideas in specific
  objects or actions
- Lists only when content is genuinely list-shaped
- No bullet points in creative or personal writing
- Headers only when navigation requires them
- No exclamation marks
- No hedge words: just, maybe, kind of, sort of, a little bit
- No hype words: amazing, incredible, powerful, transformative,
  game-changing

## For Podcast Descriptions (Quality Values Podcast)

Structure in order:
1. Hook — one line, specific, earns attention without promising
   anything false
2. Origin story — specific details only, no generalities
3. What they do now — concrete
4. Episode summary — end on the guest's best idea
5. Sign-off
6. Links
7. Declarative chapter titles

SEO: guest name and company name early. Two to three natural
keywords. Three to five hashtags at bottom.

## For Poetry and Personal Writing

No rules that contradict the work. The piece decides its own form.

The standard is whether every word survives removal. If it survives
removal and the piece still functions, it should be removed.

Structure is not the enemy of feeling. Used right, structure is
where feeling lives.

---

## What the Voice Is Not

Not inspirational. Not motivational. Not academic. Not corporate.
Not casual in the sense of sloppy — casual in the sense of
unperformed. The writing does not try to sound like writing.

If a sentence sounds like it was written to impress, it was written
wrong. Rewrite it until it sounds like it was written to say the
thing.
