## The Notebook Workflow

Jake creates in physical notebooks and documents — sketches, ideas,
plans, refinements. He does not live in a terminal or an IDE. His
creative work happens on paper first.

Periodically he photographs notebook pages and documents and submits
them to agents for processing. This is the primary input mechanism.

**The check-in cycle:**
1. Jake submits photographs of notebook pages and/or documents
2. Agents process the content — extract ideas, identify actionable
   items, flag ambiguities, connect to active projects
3. Agents queue proposed actions in PROJECTS.md notebook queue
4. Jake reviews, clears ambiguities, approves sensitive actions
5. Agents execute approved work

Between check-ins, agents work in the background — building,
refining, maintaining — on whatever has been approved and is
unambiguous. The system should never be blocked waiting for Jake
unless a genuinely ambiguous or sensitive decision is required.

The goal over time: check-ins become shorter and less frequent
because the system understands more and needs less clarification.
Each check-in closes gaps in the documentation. The documentation
gets more complete. The agents get more autonomous.
