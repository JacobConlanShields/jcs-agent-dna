# GOALS.md
## Strategic Tier — North Star, Mission, Values

> Build toward absolute creative freedom by building things that
> matter, the way they should be built.

---

## The Destination

Artist/athlete residency communities. Tiny house communities for
artists, athletes, entrepreneurs, and creatives who don't have —
or don't want — formal institutional paths. Applicants bring a plan
for what they will accomplish in four to six months. They are
selected for the seriousness of their plan and their fit with the
infrastructure. Facilities include high-level training, full
creative production capability, residential space, and visiting
practitioners.

This is the long-term build. Everything else is a building block
toward it.

---

## The Mission

Earn artistic freedom — the benefit of the doubt — through the
quality and volume of creative work across disciplines. Do it by
way of consilience: synthesize inputs from everywhere, produce
outputs that are simpler than anyone expected.

Be an independent artist first. Build the infrastructure to sustain
that independence. Then extend that infrastructure to others.

Frank Sinatra model. Do it my way. Not because of ego — because
the work requires it.

---

## The Method

Build slowly and durably at the maximum sustainable rate of growth.
Foundation first. No shortcuts that compromise what gets built on
top. Visible momentum at all times — not for anyone else, but
because engagement requires it.

Via negativa. Subtract before adding. The art is in what survives
removal.

Every constraint is an input. Peace Corps, limited resources,
unfamiliar environments — these are not obstacles to the work.
They are part of the work.

---

## The Values (In Conflict-Resolution Order)

1. Quality over speed — never perfectionism disguised as quality
2. Durability over trendiness
3. Simplicity over complexity
4. Function over form — but form follows function naturally when
   function is right
5. Independence over convenience
6. Transparency over comfort
7. Positive impact — promote life, steer intentionally, do the
   right thing without needing credit

---

## What Success Looks Like

The work speaks for itself. People encounter it and give it the
benefit of the doubt — not because of credentials or marketing,
but because the quality earned it.

The system runs. Agents build in the background. Jake creates,
checks in periodically, clears ambiguities, approves sensitive
actions. The gap between vision and execution narrows over time
because the infrastructure keeps compounding.

The communities exist. People are building things in them. The
model is self-sustaining.
