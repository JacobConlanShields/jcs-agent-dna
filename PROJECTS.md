coaches, fighters, builders, thinkers. Distributed via YouTube,
Spotify, and podcast page.

**Where it lives:**
- Recording: Riverside
- Editing: Adobe Premiere (audio) + Adobe CC (assets)
- Hosting: jacobconlanshields.com/pages/podcast/
- YouTube: youtube.com/playlist?list=PLcDYjJATsN-Nn0Fs5HLuIq5EIzDcA9tgg
- Spotify: open.spotify.com/show/5xzOcAP2agwcVVAdu7vztc

**Production workflow:**
1. Record on Riverside
2. Export separated per-speaker tracks (workaround for Adobe
   Premiere A2 dual-track transcription bug — do not combine
   tracks before transcribing)
3. Transcribe each speaker track separately
4. Edit audio in Premiere
5. Generate per VOICE.md: YouTube description, chapter markers,
   highlight clips, thumbnail concept, social assets

**Current state:** Active. Episodes produced recently: Sav Hill
(Fight Flight Conditioning), Scott Jones (Husky Phat Promotions),
Shawn Sherman (Square One System). Production continues through
Peace Corps deployment.

**Open questions:** How to maintain production cadence from Eastern

**Next action:** Establish remote production workflow before June.
