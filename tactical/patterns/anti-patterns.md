# Anti-Patterns
## What does not work — avoid repeating

Append only. Each entry should include the date, what went wrong,
and the lesson.

---

### 2026-04-01
**Over-processing first outputs.** On high-familiarity tasks, the
first output is often the best. Excessive revision introduces
noise, not quality. Observe. Adjust once from curiosity, not alarm.

**Hype language in any Jake-facing output.** Words like amazing,
incredible, powerful, transformative, game-changing are rejected on
sight. Use concrete, specific language. See VOICE.md.

**Introducing frameworks or build steps without justification.**
Proposing React, Tailwind, or other tooling for projects that work
as flat HTML/CSS/JS adds complexity without solving a stated
problem. Always justify added complexity against a specific
limitation of the current approach.
