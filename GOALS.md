## Goal 2: Artist/Athlete Residency Communities

**Definition:** Small communities where artists, athletes,
entrepreneurs, and creatives without formal institutional paths
can apply to come for four to six month stays. They bring a plan.
The space provides infrastructure, resources, and distinguished
minds in residence. The credential they leave with is what they
built.

**Win conditions:**
- Own or control land suitable for small residential community
- Architectural capability to design and oversee tiny house builds
- Land management knowledge to operate sustainably
- Project management systems capable of running the community
  without constant creative input
- Sufficient financial independence to fund the build without
  compromising creative freedom
- First cohort selected and in residence

**Current gap:**
- No land
- Architectural skills insufficient — need development
- Land management understanding insufficient — need development
- Project management at this scale untested
- Financial runway not yet established
- Proof of concept exists only at smallest scale: the bus

**Next actions:**
- Identify what architectural knowledge is specifically needed
  and find the fastest path to it
- Research land management fundamentals relevant to small
  residential communities
- Continue building financial independence through creative
  and business outputs
- Use Peace Corps deployment as an input source — observe
  community design, resource management, and infrastructure
  in a resource-constrained environment
